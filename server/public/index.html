Welcome! One-time setup

Reading your Steam library needs your own free Steam Web API key. It's stored encrypted on this computer and only used to talk to Steam.

  1. Open steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey and sign in.
  2. For "Domain Name", enter localhost, agree to the terms, and click Register.
  3. Copy the key it shows and paste it below.
